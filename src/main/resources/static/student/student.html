Essential Examination Protocols

Violations may lead to automated session flags or administrative termination.

🔒

Sequential Section Progression

Students cannot jump ahead to Section 2 until all Section 1 questions are completed or the section timer runs out.

⏱

Server-Authoritative Section Timers

Timers run on the server clock. Refreshing or closing the tab will NOT reset the timer. Expired sections auto-submit.

💾

Real-Time Database Persistence

Every answer and status is saved to the server on "Save & Next". If your internet drops, log in again to resume seamlessly.

📹

AI & Proctored Video Logging

Webcam and mic feeds are verified. Tab switches and screen blur events are counted and logged on the admin dashboard.
